UML diagrams for Sunrise Dental Clinic

CIS6003 Advanced Programming – Task A

Open this file in Chrome or Edge. Press Ctrl+P and save as PDF, or screenshot each figure and paste it into Word. Extra sequence files (login, search, help, logout) are in the same folder as .puml files. Use-case include and extend rules are written under Figure 1 and in the Word report.

Assumptions I made for these diagrams

  1. Reception and admin are different actors because they do not get the same menu after login.
  2. The appointment number is generated by the system. Staff do not type it.
  3. A patient can have many appointments. One appointment has at most one bill.
  4. The consultation fee comes from the clinic_settings table, not from a hardcoded value.
  5. Staff must log in before they can register, search or bill. Logout is the “exit” option on the website.
  6. I included the patient actor because the system also has an online booking portal. That is an extra feature, not in the original six-point list.
